4. About Hypnotherapy

4-1 Definition

Hypnotherapy is a complementary wellness approach that works with the subconscious mind through a state of deep relaxation, supporting change in patterns of thought, feeling, and behaviour.

It encompasses a range of approaches, including inner child work, grief hypnotherapy, past life regression, and self-suggestion.

4-2 Not a medical service

Hypnotherapy is not a medical service, psychiatric treatment, or a substitute for psychotherapy. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent illness, and is positioned as complementary to existing medical care.

4-3 Safety

You remain conscious throughout the session and may end it at any time. The practitioner will not do anything contrary to your wishes. Please feel at ease.
