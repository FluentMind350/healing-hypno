2 What Hypnotherapy Is

Hypnotherapy is a complementary approach that uses a relaxed, focused state to support your own awareness and change.

The practitioner does not "control" you. Throughout the session you remain able to choose your own actions, and you may open your eyes or stop at any time.

Hypnotherapy is not a medical service, a form of psychotherapy, or psychiatric treatment. It does not involve diagnosis or changes to medication, and does not replace existing medical or psychological care.

4 Contraindications

Please discuss with the practitioner and your treating physician in advance if any of the following apply. Sessions may be declined, or medical approval may be required.

  • History of epilepsy or seizure disorders
  • Diagnosis of schizophrenia, dissociative disorders, or other conditions affecting reality testing
  • Severe depression, suicidal ideation, or history of self-harm
  • Pregnancy (some suggestions and breathing techniques may not be suitable)
  • Currently receiving treatment with psychotropic medication
  • Serious cardiac conditions
  • Being under the influence of alcohol or drugs

5 Online (Remote) Sessions

5.1 Connection and environment

  • Please arrange for yourself a stable internet connection and a quiet, private space.
  • Where a session is interrupted by connection failure or similar, how this is handled (resuming, rescheduling, refund) follows the Terms of Use.
  • Because sessions involve entering a hypnotic state, they must not be undertaken while driving or operating machinery.

5.2 Recording

Sessions are not recorded.

Where, in the course of the work, the practitioner judges that a recording would be of benefit, it may be suggested — for instance, where listening back at home to the suggestions given in a session seems likely to help.

Any recording requires your consent on that occasion. Declining has no effect whatsoever on the content or quality of your sessions.

Where a recording is made, it is held by the practitioner in a password-protected environment and deleted within one month. Where it is judged necessary to retain it for ongoing work, it will in no case be held beyond six months. It will be deleted promptly at any time on your request.

6 Referral to Other Professionals

Where medical or psychiatric concerns arise during a session that fall outside the practitioner's scope, the session may be paused and you may be advised to consult a doctor, psychiatrist, or other specialist.

Please understand that this is done with your safety as the first priority.

7 Risks and Limitations

  • Responses vary between individuals. No particular outcome is guaranteed.
  • Temporary drowsiness, heightened emotion, or the surfacing of past memories may occur after a session.
  • Hypnotherapy includes areas where established medical evidence is limited. It is positioned as a complementary approach.

8 Confidentiality and Data
